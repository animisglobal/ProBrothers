## Egress Windows & Permits

Pennsylvania code requires at least one egress window in any finished basement that includes a sleeping room (bedroom). Even for non-bedroom finished basements, egress windows are strongly recommended for fire safety and required for full certificate of occupancy in most Delaware County municipalities.

ProBrothers installs egress window wells — cutting the foundation wall, installing a steel well, and framing the window opening. Egress window installation in Delaware County typically costs **$2,500–$4,500 per window** including excavation and waterproofing. We handle the permit application and the inspection through to sign-off.

### What's the difference between basement finishing and basement remodeling?

**Basement finishing** converts an unfinished, raw basement into livable space — framing, insulation, drywall, flooring, lighting, from scratch. **Basement remodeling** updates or reconfigures an already-finished basement — new flooring, adding a bathroom, converting an open rec room into defined spaces, or updating a dated wet bar.
