## FAQ: Bathroom Remodeling in Media, PA

### How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Delaware County, PA?

A bathroom remodel in Delaware County, PA typically costs between **$8,000 and $35,000**. A basic refresh — new vanity, tile, and fixtures — runs $8,000–$15,000. A full bathroom remodel with new tile, shower enclosure, vanity, and lighting runs $15,000–$25,000. A luxury master bath remodel with custom tile, soaking tub, and frameless glass enclosure runs $25,000–$35,000+. ProBrothers provides a free detailed quote for every project within one business day.

### How long does a bathroom remodel take in Media, PA?

Most bathroom remodels in Media, PA take **3 to 6 weeks** from demolition to final walkthrough. A basic cosmetic update (tile, vanity, fixtures) takes 2–3 weeks. A full gut renovation — especially one requiring new plumbing or electrical — takes 4–6 weeks. Timeline depends on material lead times, permit approval, and project scope. ProBrothers provides a written project schedule at the start of every job.

### Do I need a permit for a bathroom remodel in Delaware County, PA?

Yes, most bathroom remodels in Delaware County require permits — specifically when the project involves moving or adding plumbing, electrical work, or structural changes. A cosmetic-only update (replacing fixtures, painting, swapping a vanity) typically does not require a permit. ProBrothers handles all permit applications and inspections for permitted projects in Media, PA and surrounding municipalities, including Springfield, Glen Mills, and Newtown Square.

### What areas does ProBrothers serve for bathroom remodeling?

ProBrothers serves homeowners across **Delaware County and the Main Line**, including Media, West Chester, Glen Mills, Newtown Square, Springfield, Allentown, Malvern, Kennett Square, Nether Providence, and Marple. ### What's the difference between a bathroom refresh and a full gut renovation?

A **bathroom refresh** replaces surface-level elements — vanity, toilet, fixtures, paint, and sometimes tile — without touching the plumbing layout or walls. Cost range: $8,000–$15,000, timeline 2–3 weeks. A **full gut renovation** tears the bathroom down to the studs, replaces plumbing, waterproofing, and electrical, and rebuilds entirely. Cost range: $15,000–$35,000+, timeline 4–6 weeks. ProBrothers can advise which scope is right for your bathroom during a free in-home consultation.
