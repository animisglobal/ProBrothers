## FAQ: Kitchen Remodeling in Media, PA

### How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Delaware County, PA?

A kitchen remodel in Delaware County, PA typically costs between **$20,000 and $80,000**. A budget refresh — new cabinets and countertops — runs $20,000–$35,000. A mid-range full kitchen remodel with new cabinets, countertops, tile backsplash, and appliances runs $35,000–$60,000. A premium custom kitchen with quartz or stone countertops, custom cabinetry, and layout redesign runs $60,000–$80,000+. ProBrothers provides a free detailed quote for every project within one business day.

### How long does a kitchen remodel take in Media, PA?

Most kitchen remodels in Media, PA take **4 to 8 weeks** from demolition to final walkthrough. A targeted refresh (new cabinets, countertops, backsplash) takes 3–5 weeks. A full gut renovation with layout changes and new plumbing or electrical takes 6–10 weeks. Custom cabinet lead times (4–6 weeks from order) are often the longest single variable. ProBrothers provides a written project schedule before work begins.

### Do I need a permit for a kitchen remodel in Delaware County, PA?

Yes, in most cases. Kitchen remodels in Delaware County that involve moving plumbing (sink, dishwasher drain), adding or relocating electrical circuits, or structural changes (wall removal) require permits. A purely cosmetic update — replacing cabinet doors, painting, swapping countertops — typically does not. ProBrothers handles all permit applications and inspections in Media, Springfield, West Chester, and surrounding municipalities so homeowners don't have to navigate the process alone.

### What's the difference between /services/cabinets/ and a full kitchen remodel?

A **kitchen remodel** is a broader scope: it includes cabinets but also countertops, backsplash, flooring, lighting, plumbing fixtures, and possibly layout changes. If you only need new cabinet boxes and doors, the cabinets service is the right fit. If you want to transform the entire kitchen, that's a full kitchen remodel.
